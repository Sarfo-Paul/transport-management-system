# Literature Review

## Overview of Transport Management Systems
Transport management systems (TMS) are integral to the efficient operation of transportation and logistics. They facilitate the planning, execution, and optimization of the physical movement of goods, both incoming and outgoing. TMS solutions provide a framework for managing transportation operations, including route planning, load optimization, and freight auditing. The evolution of TMS has been driven by the need for improved efficiency, cost reduction, and enhanced visibility in supply chain operations.

## Review of Existing Transport Systems
Several existing transport management systems have been developed, each with unique features and functionalities. Notable systems include:

1. **SAP Transportation Management**: A comprehensive solution that integrates with other SAP modules, offering advanced analytics and reporting capabilities.
2. **Oracle Transportation Management**: Provides a robust platform for managing transportation logistics, with features for planning, execution, and freight payment.
3. **Transporeon**: A cloud-based platform that connects shippers and carriers, focusing on real-time visibility and collaboration.

These systems demonstrate the importance of integrating technology into transport management, yet they often come with high costs and complexity, making them less accessible for smaller organizations.

## Technological Frameworks for Web-Based Systems
The development of web-based transport management systems has been facilitated by advancements in various technological frameworks. Key frameworks include:

- **PHP and MySQL**: Widely used for server-side scripting and database management, allowing for dynamic content generation and data storage.
- **JavaScript Frameworks (e.g., React, Angular)**: Enhance user experience by providing interactive and responsive interfaces.
- **RESTful APIs**: Enable seamless communication between different system components and third-party services, promoting modularity and scalability.

These frameworks support the development of robust, scalable, and user-friendly transport management systems.

## Gap Analysis
Despite the advancements in existing transport management systems, several gaps remain:

- **Affordability**: Many solutions are priced beyond the reach of small to medium-sized enterprises (SMEs).
- **Customization**: Existing systems often lack flexibility, making it difficult for organizations to tailor functionalities to their specific needs.
- **User Experience**: Some systems have complex interfaces that can hinder user adoption and efficiency.

## Justification for the TransPass UG Project
The TransPass UG project aims to address the identified gaps by providing an affordable, customizable, and user-friendly transport management system. By leveraging modern web technologies and focusing on the specific needs of users, TransPass UG seeks to enhance operational efficiency and improve the overall transport management experience for its users. This project not only fills a critical gap in the market but also contributes to the ongoing evolution of transport management systems by incorporating best practices and innovative solutions.
