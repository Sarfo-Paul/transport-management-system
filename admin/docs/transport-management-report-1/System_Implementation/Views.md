# Views in the Transport Management System

## Overview
The Views component of the Transport Management System (TMS) provides a user-friendly interface for interacting with various functionalities of the system. This document outlines the optional views available within the system, detailing their purpose and how they enhance user experience.

## Optional Views

### Driver Assignments
The `driver_assignments` view simplifies the management of driver trip lists. It allows administrators to easily assign drivers to specific trips, view their schedules, and monitor their availability. This view is particularly useful for optimizing resource allocation and ensuring that all trips are adequately staffed.

#### Features:
- List of all active drivers and their current assignments.
- Ability to filter assignments by date, route, or driver.
- Quick access to driver profiles for additional information.

### Passenger Bookings
The `passenger_bookings` view provides users with a streamlined interface to manage their bookings. This view allows passengers to view their current and past bookings, make changes, or cancel reservations as needed.

#### Features:
- Display of all bookings associated with the user.
- Status indicators for each booking (e.g., confirmed, pending, cancelled).
- Options to modify or cancel bookings directly from the view.

## Benefits of Using Views
Implementing these optional views enhances the overall functionality of the Transport Management System by:
- Improving user engagement through intuitive interfaces.
- Streamlining administrative tasks, reducing the time required for managing assignments and bookings.
- Providing clear visibility into the status of drivers and passengers, facilitating better decision-making.

## Conclusion
The optional views in the Transport Management System play a crucial role in enhancing user experience and operational efficiency. By providing tailored interfaces for both drivers and passengers, the system ensures that all stakeholders can effectively manage their interactions with the transport services offered.
